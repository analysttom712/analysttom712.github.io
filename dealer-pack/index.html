Monthly financial pack: equipment dealer

A sample of the reporting I'd build for a budget and forecasting team: budget versus actual by department and region, a year-end forecast, dealer KPIs, a what-if page, data checks and a report catalog.

Sample data, fictional dealer. Every number is made up. Not affiliated with any equipment maker or dealer. Actuals run January to October 2026; November and December are forecast.

    Department P&L: budget versus actual

    Flag: variance over 5% and over $50K either way. Overhead is fixed operating cost (facilities, salaried staff, systems).

      Report catalog

      A starting catalog of the recurring reports, who uses them and when they're due.

      How it's built

      • Data: 12 months × 3 regions × 4 departments, plus overhead, service hours, rental fleet time and receivables, generated with a fixed seed so the numbers never change between visits.
      • Forecast: November and December budget × each department's year-to-date attainment (actual ÷ budget) in that region. Gross margin uses the year-to-date actual margin.
      • Absorption rate: (parts gross profit + service gross profit) ÷ overhead. At 100%, parts and service cover the fixed costs before a single machine is sold.
      • Service tech utilization: billed hours ÷ available hours. Rental time utilization: days on rent ÷ days in the fleet. DSO (days sales outstanding): receivables ÷ revenue × days in the month.
      • Tools: I normally build this in Power BI with Power Query and DAX. This web version shows the same logic without needing Power BI. Claude (AI) wrote the HTML and JavaScript at my direction; I set the KPIs, formulas, forecast method and checks, and reviewed every result.
